The Procurement module transforms the bidding process from a frustrating guessing game into a structured comparison of clear proposals.

## The problem with typical bidding

When you ask for quotes on a self-build, you typically get:

- **Vague scope** — "Complete framing" means different things to different builders
- **Hidden exclusions** — What's not included only becomes clear after signing
- **Arbitrary allowances** — "$5,000 for fixtures" with no basis in reality
- **Incomparable numbers** — You can't compare a $180k quote to a $220k quote if they don't include the same things

## How structured procurement helps

Our bid packages define:

- **Exactly what's included** in each trade's scope
- **Exactly what's excluded** and who's responsible
- **Specific material selections** or defined allowances with clear specs
- **Required documentation** so you know what you're getting

The result: You can actually compare bids because everyone is pricing the same scope.
