The Climate Resilience module ensures your home is built for the actual conditions it will face — not generic national standards that don't account for northern realities.

## Northern BC is different

Building in the north means:

- **Extended cold** — not just a few cold days, but months of sustained cold requiring robust building envelopes
- **Power reliability** — grid power may be less reliable, and outages during cold snaps are dangerous
- **Wildfire risk** — many sites are in the wildfire interface zone requiring specific construction approaches
- **Remote access** — emergency services may be far away, making self-reliance more important

## Three resilience tiers

**Baseline** — Meets code requirements with good cold-climate practice. Suitable for urban/suburban sites with reliable grid power.

**Enhanced** — Improved envelope, backup heating, and basic power resilience. Suitable for rural sites or those wanting better efficiency and comfort.

**Resilient+** — Extended off-grid capability, premium envelope, wildfire-rated construction. For remote sites or those prioritizing maximum self-reliance.
