The Finance module is designed to give you clarity on costs before you commit to building. Too many projects fail because the financial picture wasn't clear from the start.

## Why this matters

In northern BC, construction costs can vary significantly based on location, access, and seasonal factors. A project in Prince George will cost differently than one in a remote area requiring winter road access. This module accounts for these regional factors.

## What sets this apart

Unlike generic construction estimating, our approach is:

- **Plan-specific** — tied to the actual plans you're considering
- **Region-aware** — reflecting BC/northern costs and conditions
- **Phase-ready** — structured to support phased construction if needed
- **Lender-friendly** — formatted for construction financing applications
