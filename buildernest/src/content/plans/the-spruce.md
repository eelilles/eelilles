The Spruce proves that small can be sophisticated. At 680 square feet, this plan delivers everything you need for comfortable daily living without wasted space.

## Compact living, done right

Every square foot in The Spruce serves a purpose. The open living and kitchen area feels surprisingly spacious thanks to 9-foot ceilings and carefully placed windows.

## Key features

**ADU-ready** — Designed to meet accessory dwelling unit requirements in most BC municipalities. Perfect for a rental unit, home office, or guest accommodation.

**Efficient layout** — The bedroom is sized for a queen bed with built-in closet. The 3-piece bathroom includes a full tub/shower. The kitchen has full-size appliances.

**Flexible foundation** — Can be built on a concrete slab or pier foundation, making it adaptable to various site conditions.

## Ideal for

- First-time homeowners looking for an affordable entry point
- Property owners wanting a rental income unit
- Couples or singles who prefer minimal maintenance
- Guest houses for larger properties

## Living smart

The Spruce includes thoughtful storage solutions: a coat closet at the entry, kitchen pantry space, and bedroom closet with organizer options. A stackable washer/dryer fits in the bathroom utility closet.
