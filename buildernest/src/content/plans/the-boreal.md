The Boreal is our flagship compact cabin, designed from the ground up for northern BC conditions. Every detail has been considered for durability, energy efficiency, and practical living.

## Design philosophy

We designed The Boreal to feel spacious despite its modest footprint. The open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area flows naturally, with large south-facing windows to capture winter sun and provide passive solar heating.

## Key features

**Climate-ready construction** — The building envelope is designed for extreme cold, with options for R-40+ walls and R-60 attic insulation. Air sealing details are specified throughout.

**Flexible mechanical systems** — Whether you're connecting to the grid or going fully off-grid, The Boreal accommodates multiple heating, electrical, and water system configurations.

**Practical layout** — The mudroom entry provides space for gear storage and boot removal. The bathroom is centrally located to minimize plumbing runs. The bedrooms are positioned for privacy.

## Living spaces

The main living area opens to a covered porch, extending your usable space during milder months. The kitchen includes a pantry alcove, and the dining area can accommodate a table for six.

Both bedrooms include closet space and are sized for queen beds. The primary bedroom has room for a small desk or reading chair.
