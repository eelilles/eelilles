The Omineca is named for the rugged region it's designed to thrive in. This is a family home built for the realities of northern living — without compromising on modern comfort.

## Designed for families

With three bedrooms and two full bathrooms, The Omineca provides space for growing families. The main-floor primary suite offers privacy for parents, while the two secondary bedrooms share a bathroom.

## Key features

**Great room living** — The open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area is the heart of the home. Vaulted ceilings and large windows create a bright, welcoming space even during dark winter months.

**Serious mudroom** — This isn't an afterthought. The mudroom includes boot storage, coat hooks, gear space, and direct access to a half bath. Essential for managing northern winters.

**Energy independence** — Designed from the start for off-grid capability. The mechanical room accommodates battery storage, inverters, and backup systems. Solar-ready roof orientation.

## Living spaces

The primary suite includes a walk-in closet and ensuite bathroom with dual sinks. Secondary bedrooms are sized for full or queen beds with generous closet space.

The kitchen features a large island with seating, pantry storage, and space for full-size appliances. A utility room off the kitchen handles laundry and additional storage.

## Site flexibility

The Omineca works on a variety of sites. Choose from slab, crawlspace, or full basement foundations. The design can be flipped or mirrored to optimize solar orientation and views.
