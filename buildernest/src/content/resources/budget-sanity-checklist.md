The most common reason self-builds exceed budget isn't construction cost increases—it's costs that were never budgeted in the first place. This checklist helps ensure you're accounting for the full picture.

## Land Costs

Your land purchase is just the beginning.

- [ ] **Purchase price**: The obvious one
- [ ] **Closing costs**: Legal fees, transfer tax, title insurance
- [ ] **Survey costs**: Often required for building permit
- [ ] **Geotechnical report**: May be required for foundation design
- [ ] **Septic assessment**: Perc test and design (if not on sewer)
- [ ] **Site clearing**: Trees, brush, debris removal

## Pre-Construction Costs

Before you break ground, you'll spend money.

- [ ] **Architectural drawings**: Either purchasing plans or custom design
- [ ] **Engineering**: Structural, plus potentially geotechnical, civil
- [ ] **Permit fees**: Building permit, development permit if applicable
- [ ] **Utility connection fees**: Power, water, gas (these add up fast)
- [ ] **Driveway/access**: May need significant work before construction begins
- [ ] **Site preparation**: Grading, drainage, staging area

## Construction Costs

The main event, but often underestimated.

### Hard Costs (The Building Itself)

- [ ] **Foundation**: Type matters enormously for cost
- [ ] **Framing and structure**: Labour and materials
- [ ] **Roofing**: Material choice affects long-term costs
- [ ] **Exterior**: Siding, trim, paint/stain
- [ ] **Windows and doors**: Quality affects energy costs for decades
- [ ] **Insulation**: Don't skimp in northern BC
- [ ] **Electrical**: Rough-in and finish
- [ ] **Plumbing**: Rough-in and finish
- [ ] **HVAC**: Heating, ventilation, potentially cooling
- [ ] **Interior finishes**: Drywall, paint, trim
- [ ] **Flooring**: Material costs vary wildly
- [ ] **Cabinetry**: Kitchen and bath
- [ ] **Countertops**: Wide range of options and costs
- [ ] **Fixtures and hardware**: Plumbing fixtures, lighting, door hardware
- [ ] **Appliances**: Often forgotten in construction budgets

### Soft Costs During Construction

- [ ] **Project management**: Your time or someone else's
- [ ] **Inspections**: Required at various stages
- [ ] **Testing**: Blower door, radon, etc.
- [ ] **Insurance**: Builder's risk during construction
- [ ] **Temporary utilities**: Power, heat, sanitation during build
- [ ] **Waste removal**: Dumpsters and disposal fees

## Contingency

This isn't optional. It's essential.

- [ ] **Construction contingency**: Minimum 10%, prefer 15%
- [ ] **Design contingency**: 5% for changes you'll want to make
- [ ] **Market contingency**: Material prices fluctuate

**Reality check**: If your budget has no contingency, you don't have a budget—you have a wish.

## Post-Construction Costs

You're not done when the building is done.

- [ ] **Landscaping**: Grading, seeding, basic plantings
- [ ] **Driveway finish**: May be gravel during construction, paved after
- [ ] **Outbuildings**: Shed, garage, shop
- [ ] **Fencing**: Property, garden, livestock
- [ ] **Deck/patio**: Often deferred but eventually wanted
- [ ] **Final grading and drainage**: Proper site drainage prevents problems

## Carrying Costs

Money spent while building, not on building.

- [ ] **Existing housing**: Rent or mortgage while you build
- [ ] **Construction loan interest**: Typically higher than mortgage rates
- [ ] **Property taxes**: Due from land purchase forward
- [ ] **Insurance**: Land, then builder's risk, then homeowner's
- [ ] **Travel**: If building remotely, trips to site add up

## The Reality Check

### Calculate Your True Budget

1. Start with your maximum comfortable investment
2. Subtract land costs and closing
3. Subtract pre-construction costs
4. Subtract contingency (15% of remaining)
5. Subtract carrying costs
6. What remains is your construction budget

### Per Square Foot Reality

In northern BC, expect these ranges (2024):

| Build Type | Cost Range per sq ft |
|------------|---------------------|
| Basic spec | $250-325 |
| Standard quality | $325-425 |
| Higher quality | $425-550 |
| Premium/custom | $550+ |

**These exclude**: Land, site work, utilities, soft costs, and finishes upgrades.

### Warning Signs Your Budget May Be Unrealistic

- No contingency allocation
- Per-square-foot estimates below regional norms
- Significant customization assumed at base plan prices
- Utility connection costs not researched
- Site work not assessed

## Using This Checklist

1. Go through every item honestly
2. Research actual costs for your area
3. When in doubt, estimate high
4. Have someone experienced review your numbers
5. If the math doesn't work, adjust scope before starting

It's better to build a smaller, well-budgeted home than to run out of money mid-construction.
